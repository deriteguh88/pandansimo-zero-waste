Apa itu Rekayasa Hijau?

What is Green Engineering?

Rekayasa hijau adalah pendekatan merancang produk, proses, dan sistem yang meminimalkan dampak negatif terhadap lingkungan sejak tahap perancangan — bukan menambal masalah setelah produk jadi. Prinsip ini sejalan dengan hierarki Zero Waste: mencegah dampak (reduce) jauh lebih efisien daripada mengatasinya setelah terjadi (recycle/disposal).

Green engineering is an approach to designing products, processes, and systems that minimizes negative environmental impact from the design stage onward — rather than patching problems after the fact. This principle aligns with the Zero Waste hierarchy: preventing impact (reduce) is far more efficient than dealing with it afterward (recycle/disposal).

Relevansi untuk Tiga Bidang Teknik

Relevance to Three Engineering Fields

Teknik Mesin

Mechanical Engineering

Merancang struktur turbin yang tahan korosi udara laut, sistem mekanis pemrosesan sampah organik menjadi pelet pakan, dan efisiensi energi pada mesin pendingin (ice maker).

Designing turbine structures resistant to sea-air corrosion, mechanical systems for processing organic waste into feed pellets, and energy efficiency in ice-making machines.

Teknik Elektro

Electrical Engineering

Mengoptimalkan sistem hibrid angin–surya, manajemen baterai penyimpanan, dan inverter agar pasokan listrik tetap stabil walau cuaca berubah-ubah.

Optimizing the wind–solar hybrid system, battery storage management, and inverters to keep power supply stable despite changing weather.

Teknik Industri

Industrial Engineering

Merancang alur kerja (sistem) pengelolaan sampah warung hingga biogas, tata letak kawasan yang efisien, dan evaluasi kelayakan ekonomi program energi.

Designing the workflow (system) for processing food-stall waste into biogas, efficient area layout, and economic-feasibility evaluation of the energy program.
