Belajar Zero Waste dari Desa Mandiri Energi Pandansimo

Learning Zero Waste from the Energy-Independent Village of Pandansimo

Situs ini memadukan edukasi prinsip Zero Waste dengan studi kasus nyata Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Hibrid (PLTH) di Pantai Baru Pandansimo, Bantul — mulai dari potensi, implementasi, hingga tantangan teknis dan solusinya.

This site combines Zero Waste education with a real case study of the Hybrid Power Plant (PLTH) at Pantai Baru Pandansimo, Bantul — covering its potential, implementation, and the technical challenges and solutions involved.
