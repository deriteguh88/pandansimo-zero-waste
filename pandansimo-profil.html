Profil & Sejarah Pantai Baru Pandansimo

Profile & History of Pantai Baru Pandansimo

Mengenal lokasi, latar belakang kerja sama, dan perjalanan program energi terbarukan di pesisir selatan Bantul.

Getting to know the location, the background of the collaboration, and the journey of the renewable energy program on Bantul's south coast.

Lokasi

Location

Pantai Baru Pandansimo terletak di Dusun Ngentak, Desa Poncosari, Kecamatan Srandakan, Kabupaten Bantul, Daerah Istimewa Yogyakarta — sekitar 33 km dari pusat Kota Yogyakarta. Kawasan ini berada di muara Sungai Progo, menjadikannya lokasi strategis untuk kombinasi energi angin, surya, dan potensi perikanan.

Pantai Baru Pandansimo is located in Ngentak Hamlet, Poncosari Village, Srandakan District, Bantul Regency, Yogyakarta Special Region — about 33 km from central Yogyakarta City. The area sits at the mouth of the Progo River, making it a strategic location for combining wind, solar, and fisheries potential.

Latar Belakang Program

Program Background

Pembangunan Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Hibrid (PLTH) di kawasan ini dimulai pada 27 Juni 2010 dan diresmikan setahun kemudian. Program ini lahir dari kebutuhan nyata masyarakat pesisir — terutama nelayan dan pelaku usaha warung — akan sumber listrik yang stabil dan terjangkau, sekaligus menjadi proyek percontohan energi terbarukan yang memadukan tenaga angin dan surya di satu kawasan.

Construction of the Hybrid Power Plant (PLTH) in this area began on 27 June 2010 and was inaugurated the following year. The program arose from the real needs of coastal communities — especially fishermen and food-stall operators — for a stable, affordable power source, while also serving as a pilot renewable-energy project combining wind and solar power in one location.

Para Pemangku Kepentingan

Stakeholders

Program ini melibatkan kerja sama lintas lembaga, yang pengelolaannya berganti tangan seiring waktu:

The program involves cross-institutional collaboration, with management changing hands over time:

  • Kementerian Riset & Teknologi (Kemenristek) — inisiator dan pengelola awal (2010–2014), bersama LAPAN dan Kementerian Kelautan & Perikanan.
  • Ministry of Research & Technology (Kemenristek) — initiator and early manager (2010–2014), together with LAPAN (the national aerospace agency) and the Ministry of Marine Affairs & Fisheries.
  • Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM) — mitra riset dan pengembangan teknologi, melalui berbagai pusat studi dan fakultas teknik.
  • Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM) — research and technology-development partner, through various study centers and engineering faculties.
  • Bappeda Kabupaten Bantul dan dinas-dinas terkait (pengairan, pekerjaan umum, pertanian, perikanan, perindustrian & perdagangan, pariwisata) — pengelola pada periode 2014–2016.
  • Bantul Regency Planning Agency (Bappeda) and related agencies (water resources, public works, agriculture, fisheries, industry & trade, tourism) — managers during 2014–2016.
  • Kementerian Energi & Sumber Daya Mineral (ESDM) — pengelola sejak 2016 hingga sekarang.
  • Ministry of Energy & Mineral Resources (ESDM) — manager from 2016 to the present.
  • Pemerintah Desa Poncosari dan masyarakat setempat — pelaku utama yang menjalankan dan merawat fasilitas sehari-hari.
  • Poncosari Village government and local residents — the primary actors who operate and maintain the facilities day to day.
Catatan: sebagian sumber menyebut kerja sama ini sebagai kemitraan UGM–Pemkab Bantul–Kementerian ESDM. Catatan lapangan di atas menunjukkan pengelolaannya sebenarnya bertahap/estafet antar-lembaga, bukan kemitraan tiga pihak sejak awal — poin ini bisa menjadi bahan analisis menarik untuk bagian Evaluasi. Note: some sources describe this as a UGM–Bantul Regency–Ministry of ESDM partnership. The timeline above shows management actually passed between institutions in stages rather than being a three-way partnership from the start — a point worth exploring further in the Analysis & Evaluation section.

Garis Waktu Singkat

Brief Timeline

2010

Pembangunan PLTH dimulai (27 Juni), diinisiasi Kemenristek bersama UGM, LAPAN, dan Kementerian Kelautan & Perikanan.

Construction of the PLTH begins (27 June), initiated by Kemenristek together with UGM, LAPAN, and the Ministry of Marine Affairs & Fisheries.

2011

Fasilitas PLTH diresmikan dan mulai beroperasi melayani kawasan pesisir.

The PLTH facility is inaugurated and begins operating to serve the coastal area.

2012

Sambaran petir menyebabkan gangguan pasokan listrik, membatasi kapasitas yang bisa digunakan pedagang.

A lightning strike disrupts the power supply, limiting the capacity available to vendors.

2014–2016

Pengelolaan beralih ke Pemerintah Kabupaten Bantul.

Management transfers to the Bantul Regency Government.

2016–sekarang

Pengelolaan berada di bawah Kementerian ESDM, dengan tantangan korosi dan efisiensi turbin yang terus dievaluasi.

Management comes under the Ministry of ESDM, with corrosion and turbine-efficiency challenges continuing to be evaluated.
