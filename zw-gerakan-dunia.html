Zero Waste International Alliance (ZWIA)

Zero Waste International Alliance (ZWIA)

ZWIA adalah organisasi nirlaba yang menyusun definisi dan standar Zero Waste yang kini dipakai luas secara global, termasuk Zero Waste Hierarchy dan prinsip-prinsip untuk komunitas serta bisnis. ZWIA juga menyelenggarakan proses sertifikasi "Zero Waste Community" bagi kota/wilayah yang berhasil menerapkan prinsip-prinsipnya secara terukur.

ZWIA is a nonprofit organization that developed the Zero Waste definition and standards now widely used worldwide, including the Zero Waste Hierarchy and principles for communities and businesses. ZWIA also runs a "Zero Waste Community" certification process for cities/regions that measurably apply its principles.

Zero Waste Singapore & Zero Waste Masterplan

Zero Waste Singapore & the Zero Waste Masterplan

Singapura, melalui National Environment Agency (NEA), menerbitkan Zero Waste Masterplan dengan visi menjadi "Zero Waste Nation" pada 2030. Fokusnya ada pada tiga arus utama: sampah elektronik, kemasan (termasuk plastik), dan sisa makanan — dipadukan dengan rekayasa infrastruktur daur ulang dan edukasi publik berkelanjutan.

Singapore, through the National Environment Agency (NEA), published a Zero Waste Masterplan with a vision of becoming a "Zero Waste Nation" by 2030. It focuses on three main waste streams: electronic waste, packaging (including plastics), and food waste — paired with recycling-infrastructure engineering and ongoing public education.

Pendekatan Singapura relevan untuk kawasan Pandansimo karena sama-sama menekankan rekayasa hijau — infrastruktur teknis (fasilitas pengolahan, energi terbarukan) yang dirancang agar sumber daya terus berputar, bukan terbuang begitu saja.

Singapore's approach is relevant to the Pandansimo area because both emphasize green engineering — technical infrastructure (processing facilities, renewable energy) designed to keep resources circulating rather than simply discarded.

Pola Umum di Berbagai Negara

Common Patterns Across Countries

  • Kebijakan larangan/pembatasan plastik sekali pakai di banyak kota besar dunia.
  • Bans or restrictions on single-use plastics in many major cities worldwide.
  • Sistem pemilahan sampah dari sumber (rumah tangga) sebagai syarat dasar sebelum daur ulang efektif.
  • Source-segregation of waste at the household level as a basic prerequisite for effective recycling.
  • Target kota/negara "Zero Waste" dengan tenggat waktu terukur, bukan sekadar slogan.
  • Measurable "Zero Waste" targets with concrete deadlines, not just slogans.
  • Kombinasi kebijakan pemerintah, insentif bisnis, dan edukasi masyarakat agar perubahan perilaku bertahan jangka panjang.
  • A combination of government policy, business incentives, and public education so behavior change lasts long-term.
